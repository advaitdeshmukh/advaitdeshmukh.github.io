Hi! I'm Advait, a Computer Science Ph.D. student at the University of Colorado Boulder, advised by [Dr. Maria Antoniak](https://maria-antoniak.github.io/) in the [CLS Lab](https://cls-lab.com). I work on language technology: the inductive biases these systems rely on, and the assumptions people make about what they're capable of. So far, I've studied what language models inherit from their training distributions ([All Entities are Not Created Equal](https://aclanthology.org/2025.starsem-1.15/)), and how people engage with them for story generation, using conversations from WildChat ([The Garden of Forking Prompts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14677)).

I am particularly interested in working with narrative and discourse. Partly because I love fiction. Partly it's that discourse is hard: coherence and diversity can be tricky to define, and in an era of RLVR, this subjectivity makes it a compelling problem.

Previously, I worked with [Dr. Maria Pacheco](https://blast-cu.github.io/mlpacheco/) at the [BLAST Lab](https://blast-cu.github.io/), interned at Amazon, and spent two years as a software engineer at Société Générale.

For fun, I like reading books, playing chess, and ~~painting~~ cat-sitting.
