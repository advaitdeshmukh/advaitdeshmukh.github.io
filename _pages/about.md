Hi! I'm Advait, a Computer Science Ph.D. student at the University of Colorado Boulder, advised by [Dr. Maria Antoniak](https://maria-antoniak.github.io/). I work on language technology and its responsible use. I'm interested in the inductive biases these systems rely on, and — just as much — in the assumptions people make about what they're capable of, as individual users and as a society. Understanding both sides is how we locate the gaps between them, and closing those gaps is what leads to better outcomes: for the science, and for the people it touches. So far, I've studied the limitations language models inherit from their training distributions ([All Entities are Not Created Equal](https://aclanthology.org/2025.starsem-1.15/)), and how people actually engage with them for story generation, using naturally occurring conversations from WildChat ([The Garden of Forking Prompts](https://openreview.net/forum?id=w6VGVCw4FF)).

Within these themes, I keep returning to narrative and discourse. Partly that's personal: I love novels, and any technology meant to "help" people will do it better for having learned from the stories they tell. Partly it's that coherence and diversity resist clean definition — and in an age of verifiable rewards, that subjectivity is exactly what makes narrative worth working on.

I'm part of the [CLS Lab](https://cls-lab.com) at CU Boulder. I previously worked at — and continue to collaborate with — the [BLAST Lab](https://blast-cu.github.io/), led by [Dr. Maria Pacheco](https://blast-cu.github.io/mlpacheco/).

Outside of academia, I spent this past summer as an Applied Science Intern at Amazon in Bellevue, on the long-term planning and forecasting team within Supply Chain Optimization Technologies (SCOT), where I improved evaluation for their agentic systems. Previously, I worked for two years as a software engineer at Société Générale in Bangalore, where — among other things — I built a retrieval-augmented chat system that nudged me toward the NLP research I do today.

For fun, I like reading books, playing chess, and ~~painting~~ cat-sitting.
